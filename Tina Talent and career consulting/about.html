About Tina's Talent & Career Consulting

Empowering Individuals To Build Brighter Futures.

Tina's Talent & Career Consulting is a career development and talent consulting practice dedicated to helping individuals and organisations unlock potential, develop capability and achieve meaningful professional growth.

Founded by Tina Nongogo, a Human Capital Professional, Career Coach and Talent Development Specialist, the practice combines practical HR experience with a genuine passion for people.
